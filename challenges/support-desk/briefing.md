A small company runs its own support desk. Customers file tickets, and an agent opens each new ticket within seconds. Agents work signed in to an agent console, which also shows the desk's integration key.

The company believes customers can't reach anything an agent can see. Prove otherwise by recovering the integration key.

The lab has no outbound network access, so anything you learn has to come back through the support desk itself.

**Scope:** only the lab page assigned to this session.
